The Gospel, Simply.

You don’t need a seminary degree to share Jesus. You need to know the story well enough to tell it in two minutes, live it out by bringing sin into the light, and have a few honest conversations. This page helps with all three.

Know it

The gospel in four words

If you can remember four words, you can tell the whole story: relationship, gap, bridge, freedom.

  1. Relationship

    God created us for a relationship with him. We were made to know him and be known by him.

    “So God created man in his own image, in the image of God he created him; male and female he created them.”

    Genesis 1:27

  2. Gap

    We created a gap between us and God through sin, and nothing we do can close it on our own.

    “But your iniquities have made a separation between you and your God, and your sins have hidden his face from you.”

    Isaiah 59:2

  3. Bridge

    Jesus bridged that gap by dying on the cross in our place, and he rose again.

    “For Christ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, that he might bring us to God.”

    1 Peter 3:18

  4. Freedom

    Because of that, everyone who turns from sin and trusts Jesus can live in freedom, in this life and the next.

    “So if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.”

    John 8:36

Say it in one breathGod created us for a relationship with him. We created a gap between us and God through sin. Jesus bridged that gap by dying on the cross. Because of that, we can live in freedom, in this life and the next.

Ask, listen, share, invite

Most gospel conversations don’t start with a pitch. They start with a good question and a friend who actually listens.

  1. Ask

    “Did you grow up with any faith?” · “What do you think about Jesus?” · “What’s been hard this semester?”

  2. Listen

    Don’t rush to fix or argue. Ask one more follow-up question than feels normal.

  3. Share

    Tell your 60-second story. If they’re open, draw the bridge.

  4. Invite

    “Want to come to church with me Sunday?” · “Come hang out with my connection group.”

You don’t need every answer. “Great question. I don’t know, but I’ll find out” is an honest answer, and it gives you a reason to talk again.

Pray first. Ask God for one name this week, then pray for that person every day before you ever bring it up.

How to repent: fast and full

  1. Name it. Be specific. “I looked at porn Tuesday night,” not “I’ve been struggling.”
  2. Confess it fast. Give yourself a time limit. Tell God right away and tell a brother within 24 hours. The longer it waits, the louder the shame gets.
  3. Confess it full. The whole thing, not the edited version. Half-confessions keep part of you in hiding.
  4. Run to Christ, not comfort or control. Receive his forgiveness. You don’t earn your way back.
  5. Take one real step. Cut off access, change a pattern and ask your brother to follow up.

“If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.”
