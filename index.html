Goals

Four areas, one life. Burnout usually isn’t from working hard. It’s from never stopping. So every area has two sides: push, then recover. God built that rhythm into creation: six days of work, then a day of rest.

“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”

Matthew 11:28

Pick a few goals in each area, including at least one marked Rest. Start small: it’s better to keep three goals than to drop ten.

The idea of training all four areas, with rest built in, comes from The Power of Full Engagement by Jim Loehr and Tony Schwartz.
